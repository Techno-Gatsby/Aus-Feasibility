Multiplied ×23 — and the alternative already works

1 sent ~23inboxes

3.8M received vs 168K sent: reading dwarfs writing, so CC lists and reply-all are the cheapest attack. Teams already carries 74% of written messages — but 2,134 people email without ever chatting, and construction entities stay email-first.

"Chat first internally; email for external & formal" pushes on an open door.

Do this

  1. Move the top 3–5 email-run workflows into systems — approvals, timesheets, POs, HR requests.
  2. Channel rule: internal → Teams; external, formal, record → email. Leadership models it.
  3. Cut the ×23: prune distribution lists, fewer CCs, no reply-all.
  4. Measure first: 4–8 weeks of this report; Viva Insights for actual hours.
Target: −20% internal email in the five heaviest units within 8 weeks, no drop in turnaround.
